Current Sage & retained resources

The current Mage uses KayKit Adventurers: Mage body and female Mage head, Ranger male head, Rogue hood, native spellbook, hat, cape, wand, staff, rig and animations. QUINTARC adapts the robe and materials. Earlier Wizard, Witch and Elf assets remain in the project’s historical license library.

Current creatures and bosses use Quaternius Ultimate Monsters with matte materials and biome colors. Earlier source packs and their modification notices remain listed below.

Spell icon & shield artwork

Spell icons combine licensed Game-icons.net silhouettes and original QUINTARC artwork. Authors, sources, licenses and modifications are recorded in the per-spell provenance and contributor notices.

Leviathan Surge uses Big wave by Lorc, CC BY 3.0, recolored cyan/white and rasterized. License and modifications.

The five personal elemental dome shields use original Wobumii geometry, procedural shader artwork, ground seals and elemental motifs. Other spell effects retain their separate source notices.

Studio & website artwork

The black-and-white Wobumii wordmark and W emblem are supplied studio artwork, used without altering the originals. The website uses a shared black-and-white brutalist theme. Game covers and portraits are displayed in monochrome while retaining their original source files.

The QUINTARC seal incorporates Open book by Lorc, CC BY 3.0, recolored and composed with five elemental orbs and symbols, surrounding rings, and a dark panel.

Gameplay screenshots are native captures of the deployed game scenes, staged to show features clearly. Item and mob portraits are Godot renders of current game geometry and the licensed models credited above. The Hole portrait uses its actual circular drawing recipe.
