The deployment

One mech against an alien horde. MECHARNAGE is a top-down sci-fi survival game set on Nysa. Move and aim while your weapons fire automatically, choose upgrades as you gain experience, and build a loadout that can survive the next wave.

Complete campaign objectives or enter endless Survival. Between runs, use salvaged Gears and Components to improve permanent Mech Parts.

Ways to play

Campaign / 30 missions

Follow sequential missions across six biomes, with distinct objectives and first-clear rewards. Deploy into bioluminescent wilds, forests, volcanic terrain, crimson highlands, frozen wastes and the Abyssal Depths.

Endless Survival

Face escalating alien pressure and timed bosses. Each arena tracks its own personal best, so a record from one location does not appear on every mission.

Weapons & enhancements

Each run has six weapon slots and six enhancement slots. Choose upgrades during deployment to install new equipment or increase its rating.

Build your arsenal

Combine the Rotary Cannon, Missile Rack, Flamethrower, Tesla Coil, Defense Drones and Shockwave Generator. Focus a weapon with 1–6.

Support the loadout

Enhancements improve systems such as cooling, payloads, fuel, energy, drone control and reactor capacity. Field Systems explains their effects and shows the current component models.

Mech progression

Six bars, then EVO

Six bars is a weapon’s normal maximum. With its matching enhancement installed at one bar or more, a separate evolution choice becomes eligible. Six bars alone is not EVO.

Permanent Mech Parts

Temporary weapons and enhancements belong to the current run. Gears and Components improve permanent Mech Parts between deployments.

Overdrive

Charge the circular gauge and press F when it is ready. Overdrive gives your mech a burst of power when the horde closes in.

Read the HUD

Hull, Shield and Overdrive sit in the top-left panel. The timer is centered above the battlefield. Weapons and enhancements sit at the bottom, above the full-width experience bar.

Install & run

Download the Linux x86_64 build from itch.io and extract the complete archive. Run launch.sh or MECHARNAGE.x86_64. Keep the executable, MECHARNAGE.pck and libmecharnage.so together.

Vulkan is the default renderer. If Vulkan is unavailable, try launch-opengl.sh. Current graphics drivers are recommended. Heavy evolved-weapon crowds can still reduce performance in this development build.

Quick answers

Are six bars the same as EVO?

No. Reach six weapon bars and install its matching enhancement to make a separate evolution choice eligible.

Where can I inspect the equipment?

Open Field Systems from the main screen. Its Weapons and Enhancements tabs show equipment models, ratings and effects.

Where are the credits?

The game’s About page and downloaded CREDITS folder include model, asset-pack, music, sound and font attribution. Credits are also listed on the itch.io page.
