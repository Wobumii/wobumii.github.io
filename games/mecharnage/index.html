01 / THE DEPLOYMENT

Hold your ground.

Fight across bioluminescent wilds, forests, volcanic terrain, crimson highlands, frozen wastes and the Abyssal Depths. Clear campaign objectives or push your limits in endless Survival.

02 / THE ARSENAL

Build your response.

Combine cannons, missiles, flamethrowers, Tesla arcs, drones and shockwaves. Fill six weapon and six enhancement slots with upgrades chosen during each run.

03 / THE EVOLUTION

Come back stronger.

Pair a six-bar weapon with its matching enhancement to unlock a separate EVO choice. Spend salvaged Gears and Components on permanent Mech Parts between deployments.

BEFORE YOU LAND

Linux development build.

Extract the complete download and run launch.sh. Keep the executable, data pack and native library together.

Vulkan is the default renderer. If it is unavailable, try launch-opengl.sh. Current graphics drivers are recommended.

Development continues. Heavy evolved-weapon crowds can still reduce performance.
