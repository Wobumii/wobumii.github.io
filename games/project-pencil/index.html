The idea

What if the thing you needed was only a sketch away? Project Pencil is a first-person drawing adventure where recognizable shapes become real objects in the world. Discover blueprints, bring your drawings to life, and use them to explore and survive.

Start with Learn to Draw: select a map, then choose Guided lesson for the five-step tutorial or Start practice for free drawing with standard recipes unlocked. Then enter The Fading World, play solo Survival or try multiplayer.

From a sketch to a solution

01 / Discover

Recognition matches predefined recipes; rough or similar shapes can still fail. Open the Sketchbook with Tab, use V for the optional tracing outline, and Z or X to change recipes. R cancels a sketch. Nearby walls, floors and characters can also carry cosmetic ink.

02 / Draw

Select your pencil with F. Hold left click and trace a recipe shape. Release and pause for half a second to finish. Close-together strokes combine into one drawing.

03 / Bring it to life

A recognized recipe creates a physical object and spends drawing energy. Draw ordinary items in clear space, Doors on walls, and circular Holes on floors. Blueprint, energy and placement rules still apply.

04 / Make it count

Collect the object with E, then use it in the world. Draw weapons, protection, ammunition, and ways through the environment.

Drawing tips

Recognition uses predefined recipes, rather than turning any image into an object. Moderate tilt and size variation are accepted. Open the Sketchbook with Tab and use V for the tracing outline. Z and X change the selected drawing. R cancels a sketch.

Parkour platforms add jumping and vaulting routes across the five maps and four dungeons, with clearer landings and corrected overlaps. Movement near walls and shelves, camera smoothness, enemy obstacle routing and blocked recovery have been improved. Wave preparation, HUD updates, animation and skill trails now do less repeated work. Weapon grips, blade attack orientation, the connected Sword tracing guide, character neck fit, shadow quality options and Story paper edges have been refined. Bomb ink spreads across neighboring supported floors with softer smoke and clear gaps over empty space; travelling projectiles use glow and trails, with flames on struck enemies.

Tools & inventory

Weapons & ammunition

Draw and collect weapons and their ammunition separately. Melee weapons have limited uses. Reload ranged equipment with the ammunition you carry.

Wearable equipment

Equip helmets, armor, boots, quivers and pouches. Customize your scribbler’s gender, character, hairstyle, skin, hair, outfit and leg colors from the menu.

Doors & floor Holes

Draw a Door near the bottom of a nearby wall. A circle on a floor creates a temporary Hole; the same circle drawn in open space is a Marble. Items need clear space away from surfaces.

Five inventory slots

Select slots with 1–5 or the mouse wheel; F returns to the permanent pencil. Ammunition stacks in the inventory. Tab opens the folder-style Sketchbook with its recipe tabs.

Four combat skills: deal damage and earn kills to fill the skill meter, then press Q with matching equipment. Slasher gives bladed weapons a moving four-second spin with extended reach and no durability cost. Smasher delivers three two-handed ground strikes. Thrower empowers projectiles for eight seconds with burning and extra damage; empowered bowling balls grow while rolling downhill, up to five times their size. Tanker adds armor and turns the shield into a damaging charge that knocks enemies back.

Weapons glow only while a skill is active, with trails visible in your own view and to other players, including while walking. Friendly effects match the gold glow; opposing teams see red. Confirmed damage appears as whole numbers: white for normal hits and red for criticals. Primary skill hits have a 25% long-run critical rate for double damage; sufficiently grown empowered bowling balls always crit. Exposed bomb-center instant kills and clawed traps also show critical feedback.

Shield bashes deal damage and charge skill energy. Empty inventory slots allow left- and right-click boxing. Poison and burn indicators appear above your inventory; voice indicators stack beside microphone status, and overhead names stay readable.

All drawing recipes are unlocked in Survival, multiplayer and practice. Battle modes no longer drop blueprint papers: defeated players drop usable drawn equipment with its remaining durability, ammunition or charges, ready for another player to collect. Story keeps its blueprint progression.

Story: The Fading World

Follow a five-map chapter through The Workshop → The Stacks → The Foundry → The Glasshouse → The Observatory. Collect the first Sword and Door blueprints in the Workshop, then draw your way into four dungeons. Clear enemies, defeat each guardian and complete the tool trial before drawing the exit Door. Named maps act as checkpoints.

The scenery gradually loses its color, becoming paper and graphite while your character retains its chosen colors and important information remains readable. Reach the Observatory’s top floor and defeat the Last Eraser to reveal its hidden Heart of Color. Collect the Heart to restore the world.

Reveal the guardians and final encounter

The four dungeon guardians are The Crypt Warlord, The Archive Yeti, The Foundry Demon, and The Glasshouse Behemoth. The finale is the Last Eraser, a huge stone titan at the Observatory rooftop edge. Dodge its warned palm smashes, leave the active pigment-absorption zone and attack its head or palms during recovery.

Dungeon guardians unlock and automatically equip Slasher, Tanker, Smasher and Thrower in that order. Each first skill unlock pauses combat for a readable blueprint card; Continue resumes play. Guided lessons start with a charged Slasher and let you try any of the four skills from Pause → Choose skill.

Ways to play

Learn to Draw & Story

Choose a map for a five-step Guided lesson or free practice, with standard recipes unlocked and faster energy recovery. Story follows The Fading World through its connected maps and dungeons.

Survival / Solo · Defense / 2–4 players

Choose a map for solo Survival or team up in Defense. Survive ten waves. Each player starts with five lives. The first four deaths bring a ten-second respawn; the fifth leaves a walking spectator soul. Wave clears do not refill lives. The final wave rallies standing players to the rooftop titan, with mounted crossbows and marble machine guns.

Clash / 2–4 per team

First to 20 eliminations, with an eight-minute round limit. A death becomes a ten-second soul interval before returning to your own court.

Purge / 4–8 players

Survive across five connected maps inside a shrinking safe zone. No respawns; eliminated players watch a survivor. The last player standing wins.

Choose Online for EOS rooms or Local Network for LAN play. Everyone needs a matching game version; the room creator runs the match and must stay connected.

Survival and multiplayer begin with a 60-second skill selection, ending early when all players choose. Defense and Clash use unique skills within each team; Purge allows repeats.

Utilities

Bash for 10 base damage and knockback, gaining skill energy. Stow on your back to use other items.

Pouch

Draw a vase outline with a narrow neck and wide, rounded body. Equip to carry up to 20 marbles.

Quiver

Equip to increase arrow storage from 1 to 20.

Box

Draw a square. A solid step or barricade lasting two minutes.

Door

Draw a rectangle with a small circle inside for the doorknob. Draw near the wall bottom; both doors snap to the floor.

Hole

Draw a circle on a nearby floor within 12 feet; an X inside is optional. Size follows your drawing; ground floors become pits. The floor and its artwork return when the Hole expires.

An optional sticky explosive. It sticks on impact; drawing size controls the blast radius.

An optional drawn trap, available through the Secret recipes setting.

Mobs & bosses

The skeleton roster has six loadouts: unarmed, staff, sword, axe, crossbow and shield. Guardians and the rooftop titan have their own encounters. Health and damage scale with the encounter.

Skeleton Minion

An unarmed skeleton that closes the distance and punches at close range.

Skeleton Rogue · Lunge

A faster sword-wielding pursuer that lunges into melee range.

Skeleton Mage · Poison

A staff caster whose ranged shot applies poison for four seconds. Use cover or sidestep.

Skeleton Warrior · Slam

Carries an axe and warns before a heavy area slam.

Skeleton Sentinel · Shield Bash

Carries only a shield. Its close-range bash knocks the target backward.

Skeleton Crossbow · Volley

Fires a spreading three-bolt crossbow volley. These bolts do not apply poison.

The Last Eraser · Titan Golem

The rooftop final boss. Avoid its palm smashes and pigment absorption, then attack its head and palms during recovery.

Reveal the named dungeon guardians

The Crypt Warlord, The Archive Yeti, The Foundry Demon, and the faster-running Glasshouse Behemoth.

Quick answers

Can I draw absolutely anything?

The game recognizes a catalog of 30 predefined recipes. Your sketch must match a known shape; it is not an unrestricted image-to-object system.

Why didn’t my drawing appear?

Check that you know the blueprint, have enough drawing energy, and followed its shape. Release the mouse and give the game half a second to recognize it.
