Your magic, your combination

Play as a Sage with a floating grimoire and five elemental orbs. Combine Fire, Water, Earth, Air and Arcane into 126 spells, store four at once, and take them through Story, Practice, Survival or competitive arenas.

Explore the narrative campaign, experiment in Practice, defend together in Survival, or take your combinations into competitive arenas.

CREATIVE WORK & AI DISCLOSURE

Story, mechanics & assets

The story and game mechanics are my own. The assets come from other creators, whose work is acknowledged on the Credits page.

AI is used only as a coding assistant. No AI-generated artwork was used.

Each spell has its own targeting, timing, movement, resource and terrain rules. Read its card before casting. Shields, cover, elemental counters and spell-specific cleanses shape how a fight unfolds.

Combine. Invoke. Cast.

01 / Choose five elements

Keys 1–5 select Fire, Water, Earth, Air and Arcane. Each press replaces the next highlighted orb. Quantities determine the recipe; selection order does not.

02 / Invoke with Q

Invoke the current five-orb recipe. The spell enters your stored loadout; invocation and casting are separate actions.

03 / Build a four-spell kit

The newest spell enters E, pushing older spells into R, T and G. Reinvoking a stored spell promotes it without duplicating it or resetting its recipe cooldown.

04 / Cast and adapt

Cast with E, R, T or G. Hold eligible ground or arcing spells to aim their landing marker, then release. Hold channels and personal shields to maintain them; release to stop.

Manage your mana

Passive mana recovers at one per second when recovery is allowed. Hold Arcane Ascension to restore mana without spending health. You move slowly and take more damage while channeling. Read each spell card for current costs and conditions; maintaining your own shield pauses mana recovery.

Your Sage

Choose male or female appearance; Normal, Caped or Robe costume; no headgear, Wizard Hat or Hood; and no equipment, a wand or a staff. Cloth and spellbook colors are customizable. Both the wand and staff use the right hand for casting.

Elemental counters & resistance

The arena matters: Fire ↔ Water and Earth ↔ Air counter each other. Arcane has no opposing element. Matching orbs strengthen your spell; orbs opposed by the arena weaken it. The effect is weighted by the five orbs in the recipe.

Five matching orbs give +25% potency; five countered orbs give −20%. For example, 3 Fire + 2 Water in a Fire arena gives +15% −8% = +7%. Other elements are neutral. Damage, recovery, shield strength and numerical buff bonuses scale with potency. Spell range, movement distance and ordinary status duration do not scale with map affinity.

Campaign bosses

Campaign bosses resist their chapter’s element by 35% (the Fire boss: 45%) and take 20% more from its opposing element. The Arcane boss has no elemental weakness. Boss control durations are 65% shorter, and push/pull/throw distance is 20% of normal. Their creature’s special status resistance remains: the dragon cannot Burn, and Freeze duration is further halved. Resistance does not make every boss immune to control.

Waves, shields & counterplay

Friendly waves pass through one another. Opposing wave fronts clash: strengths within 10% cancel both waves; otherwise the stronger wave continues with the weaker wave’s damage subtracted. Cover and resistance limit displacement.

Hold personal Aegis spells to maintain their smooth elemental dome, then release to stop. Each element has its own artwork; the caster can see through the interior. Shield upkeep and blocked hits spend mana and pause the caster’s recovery. Shared or directional shields follow their own spell-card rules.
