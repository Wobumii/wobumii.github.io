ABOUT ME / THE PERSON BEHIND WOBUMII

A designer’s eye.
A player’s heart.

Web DesignerUI/UX DesignerProduct Manager

Hi, I’m Anthony, the founder of Wobumii.

I started as a Web Designer, helping small and medium businesses bring their brands online. As a UI/UX Designer, I focused on healthcare systems. Today, I’m a Product Manager working on healthcare systems that help nursing facility directors, doctors, and nurses manage their daily operations.

I’ve always loved creating interfaces because I love making things people can actually use. Games bring that same drive together with something I’ve loved for years: imagining worlds, exploring possibilities, and giving people something to play.

Ideas I’ve held onto for years.

The rise of AI has opened a door for me. Game ideas I once lacked the skills or resources to build are now within reach. I still have to learn, make decisions, test, and put in the work—but I finally have another tool to help bring those ideas to life.

MY TAKE ON AI

A tool is only as good as the work you put into it.

Using AI doesn’t automatically make something low quality. The result depends on how the person uses the tool. AI doesn’t read minds or guarantee the result you imagined. Its output needs clear direction, judgment, iteration, and testing.

Like a programming language or framework, AI is a tool. If the result is careless or unusable, the responsibility sits with the person directing and shipping it. What matters to me is the care behind the work and the experience it gives the people using it.
